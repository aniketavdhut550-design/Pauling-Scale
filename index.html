The Pauling scale is used to measure the electronegativity of chemical elements. It shows how strongly an atom attracts shared electrons in a chemical bond.
Electronegativity is the ability of an atom to attract shared electrons towards itself in a chemical bond.

Fluorine has the highest electronegativity on the Pauling scale.
An ionic bond is formed when electrons are transferred from one atom to another.

Example: NaCl
A covalent bond is formed when two atoms share electrons with each other.

Examples: H₂, Cl₂ and CH₄.
A polar covalent bond occurs when electrons are shared unequally between two atoms due to their difference in electronegativity.

Example: H–Cl
A non-polar covalent bond occurs when electrons are shared equally between atoms.

Examples: H₂ and Cl₂.
